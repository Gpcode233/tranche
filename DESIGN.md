# Tranche design

Style reference: MindMarket (mindmarket.com), from the style sheet supplied by the owner. Same
family of cream paper, huge Inter, pill chrome and flat illustration; original characters and copy.

**Big idea: your index is a garden.** Stocks are hedges. One that grows past your line turns yellow
and gets trimmed. Sharing is passing on a cutting. The chart uses the same hedges as the art.

- **Colour:** cream `#f5f1e4`, white panels, ink `#2c2e2a`. Grass `#8ed462` is brand and main action.
  Yellow `#f5e211` means "past the line" or "paused". Deep green for the weekend. Sky, lilac, pink and
  teal only identify stocks or appear in art. No orange, coral or navy: those belong to Pulse.
- **Type:** Inter 400/500 only. Page headlines to 144px at -0.06em, sections to 81px.
- **Shape:** pills for buttons and nav, 28px panels on phones and 40px above. No shadows, no gradients.
- **Art:** made in Gemini, in `public/art`. `scripts/art.mjs` cuts a flat background to transparent.
- **Copy:** grow, trim, feed, cutting, seeds. Curators are fictional and say so. No invented users,
  returns or audits.
